How the prices were checked

Every deal on this page is a real listing from a real store. Prices were read from the stores' own Black Friday pages and from deal roundups that cite them (Tom's Guide, 9to5Toys, The Verge, Nintendo's own retail offers, and Japanese deal trackers) on 27 November 2026. The "was" price is the store's own regular or list price. Prices move fast during Black Friday week — always confirm the price on the store page before you buy.

Currency conversion uses a fixed rate of ¥150 = $1 and is approximate; stores charge in their own currency. Deals are shown in both currencies so US and Japanese shoppers can compare at a glance.
